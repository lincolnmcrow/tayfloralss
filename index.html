The story behind Tay Florals

Hi, I'm Naba — but you can call me Tay.

Tay Florals started from scratch in March 2024 — no floral background, no shortcuts, just creativity, faith, and a lot of heart. I'm based in the Dallas/Fort Worth area and every arrangement is built around the person receiving it.

Flowers can say love, celebration, grief, gratitude, or simply “I see you.” That's what I want every bouquet to communicate.

“Every bouquet is designed one petal at a time, just for you.”

Questions

Before you order.

Do I buy a bouquet directly on the website?

No. The custom order form is an inquiry only. Tay Florals reviews the request first so availability, timing, and price can be confirmed before payment.

Can I request a specific color or theme?

Absolutely. Include colors, favorite flowers, a theme, character inspiration, or a photo/link to an arrangement you like.

Do you offer delivery?

Local DFW delivery is available depending on location and date. Include your delivery details in the inquiry.

Can I add gifts to my bouquet?

Yes. Ask about plushies, balloons, chocolates and candy, custom cards or messages, vase upgrades, and other ideas.
