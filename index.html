The Story Behind Tay Florals

Hi, I'm Naba — but you can call me Tay.

Tay Florals started completely from scratch — no floral background, no shortcuts, just an idea, a lot of creativity, and even more faith in myself. I'm based in the Dallas/Fort Worth area, and I've been doing this since March 2024.

I'm a pretty emotional, deep person — I feel things fully, and I think that's exactly why flowers found me. They say what words sometimes can't: love, celebration, grief, gratitude, or just "I see you." Every arrangement I make carries a piece of that.

Building this hasn't been easy — there's been stress, tears, late nights, and moments I doubted myself. But there's been so much growth too, and I truly wouldn't be here without the people who believed in me along the way. Tay Florals isn't just a business to me — it's something I built with my own two hands, my heart, and the people who never stopped supporting me.

Every bouquet is designed one petal at a time, just for you.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering & Timing

How much notice do you need for a custom order?

All orders require a minimum of 3 days' advance notice, so I have time to source specific colors or flower varieties from local wholesalers.

What about weddings or large events?

Large events and weddings need a minimum of 2–4 weeks notice for bulk orders, custom color palettes, and venue coordination.

Delivery & Shipping

Can you ship fresh flowers to another state?

No — fresh flowers only travel well locally, so those are delivery or pickup only in the DFW area. Eternal flower arrangements can ship anywhere.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a nonrefundable deposit is required to begin custom orders — I'll go over exact details once we've talked through your vision.

¿Hablan español?

¡Sí! Puedes escribirme en español en cualquier momento, por mensaje o en el formulario de pedido.

Gifting & Add-Ons

Are bouquets only for girlfriends or wives?

Not at all! Plenty of my bouquets are built for guys too — favorite colors, teams, and hobbies all translate beautifully. Flowers are for anyone you want to celebrate.

What add-ons can I include?

Popular add-ons include plushies, balloons, chocolates & candy, a custom card, LED lights, a favorite drink or snack, and vase upgrades — just mention it in your custom order.
